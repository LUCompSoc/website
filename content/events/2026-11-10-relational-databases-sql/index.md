Relational databases are an extremely important topic in Computer Science. As one of the most widely used types of application databases, they play a key role in software of all sizes. Understanding how to use SQL effectively is essential to getting the most out of your database.

We'll be looking at the concept of Relational Databases, their advantages and their trade-offs before taking a practical dive into SQL to retrieve and store data as part of a real-world application
