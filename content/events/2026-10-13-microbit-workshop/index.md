This educational workshop focuses on introducing the [micro:bit](https://microbit.org/), a micro-controller built to teach kids how to code, developed by the BBC & Lancaster University!

We will give a simple overview of the device, before showing how to use [MakeCode](https://makecode.microbit.org/) to program it using a visual editor.

There will be multiple projects of varying difficulty which you can attempt. Helpers will be walking around to assist.
